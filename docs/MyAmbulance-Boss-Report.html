MyAmbulance: Your Own Car Becomes an Emergency Ambulance

Summary

MyAmbulance - your own car becomes an emergency ambulance, with one press.

In a medical emergency, the car standing in your driveway can reach the hospital faster than any ambulance - but it has no siren, no lights and no way to tell anyone what happened. MyAmbulance fixes this. The moment someone presses one big button, the family car turns on its siren, red and blue lights and a beep, navigation starts, the family's own doctor is alerted instantly with the patient's full medical history, one family member is called automatically, and the national helplines are one tap away. It is a simple, low-cost system that uses the car a family already owns, works in any phone browser, and keeps the people who matter informed in the first critical minutes.

1. The Problem in India

  • The 108 ambulance can be very late. It often takes 20 to 30 minutes to reach the door, and in those minutes the patient's condition can get much worse.
  • Traffic and narrow lanes. In big cities and small towns, the ambulance gets stuck - in an emergency, every single minute counts.
  • The family does not know the patient's medical details. Which illness, which medicines, which allergies, and which doctor to call - nobody remembers in a panic.
  • The family doctor finds out last. The doctor who has treated the patient for years is usually told much later, or not at all.
  • Private ambulances are expensive and slow, and the family car is sitting unused at home.
  • At the hospital, there is no medical history in the first critical minutes, so treatment starts from zero and is delayed.

2. Our Solution

One press of the SOS button, and the family car becomes a responding emergency ambulance.

The moment SOS is pressed, all of this happens at once:

  • Siren, red and blue lights and a beep switch on in the car, so the road opens up, and navigation starts automatically.
  • The family doctor is alerted instantly and receives the patient's complete medical history.
  • One family member is called automatically, so the whole family knows immediately.
  • 108, 112 and WhatsApp are one tap away.
  • The doctor can approve the case, or forward it to another doctor, in a single tap.

3. How It Works - For a Normal User

  1. Sign in. Just type your 10-digit mobile number. No password, no one-time code - the number itself is your login.
  2. Add your family. Enter each family member's age, blood group, illnesses, allergies and emergency contacts, and choose your family doctor.
  3. Add your car. Register the car and its small emergency device kit.
  4. Press SOS in an emergency. Your live location and the patient's health details are sent instantly.
  5. Help starts. The car's device kit switches on - siren, lights, beep and navigation - while the doctor responds.

4. What Is Built and Working Today

  • SOS dashboard - one large SOS button with quick action buttons around it.
  • Family members with health records - age, blood group, illnesses, allergies and more.
  • Choose your family doctor from inside the app - that doctor is alerted first.
  • Emergency contacts - the app calls your family members automatically.
  • Car device kit - siren, red and blue lights, beep and navigation.
  • Doctor console - the doctor can approve, forward or close a case in one tap.
  • Helplines - 108, 112 and 1091 always one tap away.
  • Auto-login with just the mobile number - no password and no one-time code to remember.

Tested end-to-end with 15 ready-to-use test accounts (5 doctors + 10 patients). Everything runs in a normal phone browser - nothing to install.
