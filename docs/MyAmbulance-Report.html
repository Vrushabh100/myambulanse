1. The Problem (India)

  • 108 ambulance bahut door hoti hai. Aane me 20-30 minute lag jaate hain, aur tab tak halat bigad sakti hai.
  • Traffic aur chhoti galiyan. Bade sheher ho ya gaon, ambulance phas jaati hai - emergency me har minute keemti hai.
  • Ghar walon ko kuch pata hi nahi hota. Patient ki condition kya hai, kaunsi dawaiyan chalti hain, kis cheez se allergy hai - kuch bhi yaad nahi rehta.
  • Patient ka apna doctor late pata chalta hai. Jo doctor saalon se ilaaj kar raha hai, use emergency ki khabar hi sabse baad me milti hai.
  • Hospital pahunchne tak family panic me hoti hai aur zaroori jaankari (blood group, purani bimari) nahi hoti, jisse ilaaj shuru karne me der hoti hai.
  • Chhote sheher aur gaon me private ambulance mehngi aur late hai, aur ghar ki car khaali khadi rehti hai.

2. Our Solution

Aapki apni car ek emergency ambulance ban jaati hai - sirf ek button se.

Emergency me bas SOS dabao - aur turant ye sab shuru ho jaata hai:

  • Siren + red/blue lights + beep aapki car par ON ho jaate hain, taaki raasta mile.
  • Navigation chalu ho jaata hai.
  • Aapke family doctor ko turant alert jaata hai - patient ki poori medical history ke saath.
  • Ghar ke ek family member ko call chali jaati hai, taaki sabko pata chale.
  • Zaroorat ho to 108 / 112 / WhatsApp - sirf ek tap par.

Doctor aapki request Approve kar sakta hai, ya kisi doosre doctor ko Refer kar sakta hai.

3. How It Works (aam aadmi ke liye)

  1. Register karo. Sirf apna naam aur 10-digit mobile number daalo. Koi OTP nahi, koi password nahi - mobile number hi aapka login hai.
  2. Family members add karo (naam, umar, blood group, bimari, allergy, emergency contacts) aur apna family doctor chuno.
  3. Car register karo. Car ka number aur uska emergency device kit daalo.
  4. Emergency me SOS dabao. Aapki live location aur patient ki health details turant bhej di jaati hain.
  5. Doctor respond karta hai aur aapki car ka device kit chalu ho jaata hai - siren, lights, beep aur navigation.

4. Why It Helps

  • Time bachta hai - ghar ki car turant nikal jaati hai, 108 ka intezaar nahi karna padta.
  • Sahi doctor ko sahi jaankari milti hai, isliye ilaaj bina der shuru ho jaata hai.
  • Family ko turant pata chalta hai aur woh panic me akeli nahi rehti.
  • Ghar ki car ka sahi use hota hai - jo pehle khaali khadi rehti thi.
  • Sasta aur turant - koi mehngi private ambulance book karne ki zaroorat nahi.

5. Who Should Use It

  • Bade-buzurg maa-baap (elderly parents)
  • Pregnant mahilayein (garbhvati mahilayein)
  • Chhote bachche wale parivaar
  • Heart ke mareez
  • Chhote sheher aur gaon ke log, jahan ambulance late aati hai
  • Har woh parivaar jinke paas apni car hai

6. Features Ready Today

  • SOS Dashboard - ek bada SOS button aur turant kaam karne wale chhote buttons.
  • Family patients + health records - umar, blood group, bimari, allergy, BP, sugar, weight.
  • Family doctor - apna doctor chuno, sabse pehle usi ko alert jaata hai.
  • Emergency contacts - parivaar ke logon ko apne aap call.
  • Car device kit - siren, red/blue lights, beep aur navigation.
  • Doctor console - doctor ek tap me Approve / Refer / Resolve kar sakta hai.
  • Helplines - 108, 112 aur 1091 hamesha ek tap par.

Under the hood: aapka data safe tareeke se online rehta hai aur app kisi bhi phone ke browser me chal jaati hai - kuch install karne ki zaroorat nahi.

8. Safety & Privacy (simple)

  • Mobile number hi login hai - koi password store nahi hota, isliye password chori hone ka dar nahi.
  • Aapka data sirf aapko dikhta hai - aapke parivaar ke records koi doosra nahi dekh sakta.
  • Doctor ko sirf apne patients ke alerts milte hain - kisi aur ka data nahi.
